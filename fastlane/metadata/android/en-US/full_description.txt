Play card games with friendly cats including Hearts, Spades, Oh Hell, and duplicate bridge. In Hearts, pass your bad cards to opponents and avoid taking hearts and the queen of spades. In Spades, bid on how many tricks you can take, then work with your partner to make your bid and prevent your opponents from making theirs. In Oh Hell, decide how many tricks you'll take and take that amount exactly. In bridge, play a duplicate teams match of 1 to 8 rounds, scored by IMPs.
