Scum With Cats

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

Scum With Cats does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. The game contains no advertising, no user accounts, and no analytics or tracking.

Overview

Scum With Cats is a free and open-source card game for Android and other platforms. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information when you use it.

Information We Collect

Scum With Cats does not collect personal information.

The application does not collect or transmit information such as:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts
  • Photos, videos, or other personal files
  • Device identifiers for advertising or tracking
  • Browsing activity
  • Advertising information
  • Analytics or usage data sent to the developer

Local Game Data

Scum With Cats stores some information locally on your device so the game can function. This can include game settings, preferences, saved game state, statistics, and other gameplay information.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to the developer or to an analytics service.

Removing the application or clearing its application data may remove this locally stored information.

Advertising

Scum With Cats contains no advertisements and does not include advertising tracking.

Analytics and Tracking

The application does not use analytics, behavioral tracking, advertising profiling, or similar tracking technologies.

User Accounts

Scum With Cats does not require or provide user accounts, registration, or login services.

Network Communication

The game itself does not require network access to play and does not send gameplay or personal information to the developer.

Third-Party Services

Scum With Cats does not embed third-party advertising or analytics services. If you obtain the application through a software distribution platform such as Google Play, that platform may independently process information according to its own privacy policy. Such processing is controlled by the platform and is not data collected by Scum With Cats.

Children's Privacy

Scum With Cats does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the application does not collect personal information from users, it does not create profiles or behavioral records for children or adults.

Open Source

The source code for Scum With Cats is publicly available. You can inspect the application and its source code at: github.com/crhy/CardsWithCats .

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if the application's features or data practices change. The current version of this policy will always be published at this address.

Contact

For privacy questions, bug reports, or other questions about Scum With Cats, please use the project's public issue tracker: github.com/crhy/CardsWithCats/issues .
